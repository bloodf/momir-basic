# Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Momir Basic is a Magic: The Gathering card randomizer app provided by [REPLACE: YOUR COMPANY NAME] ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how that information is used.

## Overview

Momir Basic does not require an account. The app does not show ads, does not offer in-app purchases, and does not use third-party analytics or crash reporting services.

## Information We Handle

### Card data from Scryfall

Momir Basic fetches Magic: The Gathering card data from the Scryfall API. When you use the app, your device sends requests to Scryfall to load card information. Scryfall is an external service and its own terms and privacy practices apply.

- Scryfall API: https://scryfall.com/docs/api
- Scryfall privacy information: https://scryfall.com/privacy

### Local data stored on your device

The app stores your draw history locally on your device only. This data is used so you can review previously generated cards inside the app.

If you use thermal printer features, the app may also store local printer-related preferences or recent printer state on your device to support printing behavior. This information stays on your device.

## Permissions

### Bluetooth

Momir Basic supports thermal printers over Bluetooth. The app may request Bluetooth-related permissions so it can discover and connect to compatible printers.

### Location on Android

On some Android versions, location permission may be requested for Bluetooth printer discovery. This permission is used only to enable nearby Bluetooth scanning required by the operating system. Momir Basic does not use location data for tracking, analytics, advertising, or profiling.

## What We Do Not Use

Momir Basic does not use the following:

- Camera access
- Photo library access
- Third-party analytics
- Third-party crash reporting, including Sentry
- Advertising SDKs

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your draw history with us or with third parties because that history remains stored locally on your device.

The only external service used for app content is Scryfall, which provides card data requested by your device.

## Children's Privacy

Momir Basic is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.
